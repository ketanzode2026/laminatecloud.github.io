Frequently Asked Questions About Laminates

Get expert answers regarding decorative laminate thickness, applications, sample delivery, and ordering from Laminate Cloud.

1mm decorative laminate sheets are high-pressure surface materials (HPL) designed with superior structural density. Unlike cheaper 0.8mm laminates, 1mm thickness prevents substrate telegraphing (waviness or glue ripples), offers high impact strength, resists heat and scratches, and remains perfectly flat across large vertical wardrobe panels.
Yes! Laminate Cloud provides a dedicated sample ordering service. You can select specific textures—such as Italian marble, synchro woodgrain, ombre, or canvas—and receive swatches directly at your doorstep to verify lighting, texture, and touch before committing to full sheets.
For modular kitchens, anti-fingerprint matte laminates and acrylic high-gloss sheets are the top choices. Laminate Cloud's kitchen surfaces are engineered with moisture barriers and thermal resistance, making them easy to wipe down and resistant to steam, oil splashes, and spices.
All laminate sheets are rolled and packed inside rigid industrial-grade wooden crates or protective roll tubes with transit insurance. Laminate Cloud delivers across all Indian states with rapid dispatch, and regularly exports full container loads to over 25 countries worldwide.
Standard laminate sheets measure 8 by 4 feet (2440mm × 1220mm). 9 by 4 feet sheets (2745mm × 1220mm) provide extra height, allowing architects and interior designers to build continuous floor-to-ceiling wardrobe shutters and wall paneling without awkward joint lines.

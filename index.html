Ombre Laminate Designs

Ombre laminates create a gradual transition between colours, giving furniture and interior surfaces a contemporary appearance.

They can work particularly well on wardrobes, vertical panels, feature walls and other surfaces where a subtle gradient can become part of the overall design.

Softer gradients can create a calm appearance, while stronger colour transitions can become a more prominent design feature.

Frequently Asked Questions About Laminates

What are decorative laminate sheets?

Decorative laminate sheets are surface materials used to add colour, texture and visual finishes to furniture, wardrobes, kitchens, wall panels and other interior surfaces.

Where can laminate sheets be used?

Laminates can be used for applications such as wardrobes, kitchen cabinets, furniture, TV units, wall panels, doors and office interiors, depending on the product specifications.

What laminate designs are available?

Laminate Cloud offers different decorative styles including woodgrain, marble, stone, matte, glossy, fluted, canvas and ombre designs.

How do I choose the right laminate?

Choose a laminate based on the intended application, design preference, surface finish, colour, texture, thickness and the manufacturer's product specifications.
